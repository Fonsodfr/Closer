=====================================================
 CLOSER for macOS
 Rave Factory · 3-band Mid/Side stereo imager
=====================================================

ENGLISH
-------

1. Double-click Closer-Installer.pkg

   The installer is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens
   straight away.

2. Install

   Continue > Install, and enter your Mac password. Done.
   (Click "Customize" if you only want the VST3 or only the Audio Unit.)

3. Open your DAW

   Restart your DAW and rescan plugins if needed.
   It appears as "Closer" by "Rave Factory".

If you installed an earlier test version by hand, delete these
first so you do not have two copies:
   ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Closer.vst3
   ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Closer.component

Works on macOS 11 or newer, Apple Silicon and Intel.


ESPAÑOL
-------

1. Haz doble clic en Closer-Installer.pkg

   El instalador está firmado y notarizado por Apple, así que se
   abre directamente.

2. Instala

   Continuar > Instalar, y escribe la contraseña de tu Mac. Listo.
   (Pulsa "Personalizar" si solo quieres el VST3 o solo el Audio Unit.)

3. Abre tu DAW

   Reinicia tu DAW y, si hace falta, vuelve a escanear los plugins.
   Aparece como "Closer" de "Rave Factory".

Si instalaste a mano una versión de prueba anterior, borra antes
estas dos para no tener el plugin repetido:
   ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Closer.vst3
   ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Closer.component

Funciona en macOS 11 o posterior, Apple Silicon e Intel.
