CLOSER for macOS - test version (Rave Factory)
=============================================

1. Copy the plugins:
   - Closer.vst3       ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
   - Closer.component  ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
   (In Finder: Go > Go to Folder... and paste the path.)

2. This test version is not signed by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it.
   Open Terminal and paste this line, then press Enter:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Closer.vst3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Closer.component

3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.
   It appears as "Closer" by "Rave Factory".

Feedback is very welcome: which DAW you use, whether it loads,
anything that sounds wrong, and what you would add.


CLOSER para macOS - versión de prueba (Rave Factory)
=====================================================

1. Copia los plugins:
   - Closer.vst3       ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
   - Closer.component  ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
   (En el Finder: Ir > Ir a la carpeta... y pega la ruta.)

   Abre el Terminal, pega esta línea y pulsa Intro:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Closer.vst3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Closer.component

3. Reinicia tu DAW y vuelve a escanear los plugins.
   Aparece como "Closer" de "Rave Factory".
